## Human Home acceptance readback

The destination Home alone now answers the approved acceptance questions:

1. **What is the game?** Telegraph-first phased puzzle combat with one Shared Player Turn Budget.
2. **What does the player repeatedly do?** Read threat → Line → Chain → Action → observe Player/Enemy Resolve → re-plan.
3. **Why do Line and Chain both matter?** Line owns Energy; Chain owns non-interchangeable Stock/Tier access.
4. **How does Shared Time create a decision?** More time spent preparing earlier leaves less active-input time for later stages; READY carries remaining time.

6. **Why is the highest Tier not always correct?** Tier is a tactical commitment band; lower Tier must retain efficiency/finishing/preservation/immediate-response cases.
7. **What does the Gatebreaker telegraph change?** It changes time/resource preparation and ATK/DEF/SUP/Tier choice; current-vs-visible-next responses differ.
8. **What does the representative 6–10 minute Slice contain?** Title → Gate Arrival → real Tutorial Turn → Gatebreaker battle → Result/Fast Retry with one Vanguard/one Gatebreaker/one Frontier Gate.
9. **What should the combat screen/visual language look like?** Telegraph and Shared Timer first, active puzzle/Action next, resources/Tier, Sidecar, Next Forecast, then spectacle; current `VISUAL-020` style.
10. **What is approved versus unproven?** Design/visual direction is documented; representative merged runtime, first-exposure Human validation, runtime readability, fun/tension and final tuning remain evidence-bounded.
